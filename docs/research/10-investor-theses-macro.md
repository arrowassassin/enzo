## D. What not to build

- **Per-seat software that analyzes public data or does a task a generic agent can do** – the SaaSpocalypse "kill zone"; Menlo's Murphy: SaaS without native agents "increasingly difficult" to fund.
- **Process-mode companies** (Cherny) and "single-purpose AI for X" tools with no data or workflow moat.
- **Crowded YC lanes**: MCP infra, docs-for-agents, browser-automation/computer-use APIs, generic agent memory; 24 of 208 S26 companies are already dev/agent tooling.
- **Another frontier lab or coding agent**; labs absorb long-horizon reasoning, world models, chip co-design, in-silico science.
- **Humanoid hardware from scratch**: 80%→99.9% reliability "requires new science"; Chinese vendors hold 97% of units and cut prices 72%.
- **Crypto-rail businesses that assume volume today**: x402 at ~$28k/day, $0.20 average, half test activity in March 2026 ([post](https://github.com/defiprime/defiprime1/blob/main/collections/_posts/2026-03-18-stripe-mpp-vs-x402.md)).
- **Climate, consumer SaaS, undifferentiated vertical SaaS**, and companies whose "ARR" is pilot revenue.
- **Self-hosting as a cost play**: it only ties DeepSeek's first-party API even at full utilization.

## E. Top 8 opportunity areas for a late-2026 start

1. **Art. 50 / agent-disclosure compliance layer** – Art. 50 live 2 Aug 2026, grace ends 2 Dec, Korea and Colorado add duties; only 2 S26 companies tag compliance. Maps to YC Fall "AI-Native Compliance Infrastructure" and Bessemer securing-agents.
2. **Proof-of-human / trust infrastructure** – Farid says he can no longer spot fakes; ElevenLabs: "authenticated real AI > authenticated real human." Maps to YC "Proving You're Human", a16z "Know Your Agent", Speedrun "tools to stay human".
3. **AI-native service firm in a licensed, slow market** (insurance brokerage, accounting, compliance, home-services back office) – Sequoia's 6x work-vs-software budgets; Avoca, Ciridae, Rebar, Manifest all funded 2026. Maps to YC "AI-Native Service Companies", Speedrun "fat AI startups own slow markets".
4. **Counter-drone and cheap interceptor software/sensing** – Army Secretary authored the YC RFS; Aurelius, ACS, Anduril in 2026; defense-robotics Series A medians 2x civilian.
5. **Robot data and evaluation, not robots** – $3B+ robot-data spend forecast; Jim Fan's World Action Model needs human video. Maps to YC "Data for the Real World", Bessemer data-moat prediction.
6. **Agent-scale infrastructure for small software** – MCP 2026-07-28 removed sessions; Cloudflare Monetization Gateway. Maps to YC "Cloud for Small Software" and "Self-Maintaining APIs".
7. **Agent commerce merchant gateway** – all five rails spec-complete in 2026; x402 Foundation launched 14 Jul; SMBs have no affordable integration path. Maps to YC crypto RFS, a16z crypto.
8. **AI for the aging population** – $500M+ VC into US eldercare 2025, demand demographic not cyclical; on-device 30B models make private always-on assistants viable. Maps to YC Fall "AI for the Aging Population", a16z prevention-health.
