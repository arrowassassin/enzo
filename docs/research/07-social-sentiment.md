## A. What dies when the labs ship

Data: the community-run "Killed by AI" graveyard ([repo](https://github.com/mixtpatrik/killedbyai), dataset [graveyard.json](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mixtpatrik/killedbyai/main/graveyard.json)) lists 111 deaths; 71 closed in 2025–26. Of those, 31 are model deprecations, and among the 25 startups/apps/hardware, the causes cluster:

| Pattern | Examples (2025–26) |
|---|---|
| **Feature parity on writing/creative wrappers** | Jasper's original platform ("collapsed as GPT-4o and Claude made its core features available for free"); Writer's GPT-wrapper era lost to native Copilot/Gemini; Reforged Labs (AI video ads for Supercell/King/Ubisoft) shut July 2026 because "foundation models got good enough for studios to build the same tooling in-house" |
| **Infra that a cloud/lab can bundle** | Tune AI (fine-tuning studio) killed when AWS/Google/Azure shipped identical tooling; Stainless SDK generator acquired by Anthropic for $300M+ and hosted product wound down |
| **Horizontal agent/automation layers** | Relay.app (Zapier-style agent automation) shut July 2026, team joined Google Chrome; OpenAI's own Operator, Atlas browser, Sora, GPT Store all killed — even lab-owned horizontal surfaces get folded into ChatGPT |
| **Consumer AI hardware** | Humane Pin (devices remotely bricked), Rabbit R1, Limitless pendant (Meta acquired, hardware ended) |
| **Regulated health AI** | Woebot, Kintsugi (7 yrs, $30M, no FDA clearance), Cydoc (7-year bootstrapped postmortem: regulatory maze + "impossibility of competing with Big Tech's free AI") |
| **Data-labeling/eval marketplaces** | Yupp.ai: 1.3M users, $33M a16z, dead in a year — "broad consumer preference signal stopped being the scarce input" |

Secondary coverage agrees on the mechanism: what was a differentiator in 2023 (templates, brand voice, file upload, memory) is now native in ChatGPT for $20 ([machinebrief](https://www.machinebrief.com/news/death-of-ai-wrapper-startups-wont-survive-2026)); NFX declared "the AI wrapper is dead" and prescribes verticalization ([NFX](https://www.nfx.com/post/ai-wrapper-dead-verticalization-startups)); a single Anthropic blog post on COBOL modernization dropped IBM 13% in a day ([alexcloudstar](https://www.alexcloudstar.com/blog/ai-wrappers-dead-what-to-build-instead-2026/)). Also note the model-churn tax on builders: GPT-4o API snapshots were force-upgraded to GPT-5.1, GPT-5.2/5.3 snapshots died in under 4 months, Gemini 3 Pro Preview lived 111 days ([graveyard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mixtpatrik/killedbyai/main/graveyard.json)).

**The rule:** anything whose value is *access to or light shaping of a frontier model's output* dies; anything horizontal enough for a lab to want dies even if it's the lab's own product; anything with heavy regulatory gating dies of slow sales cycles. Survivors are deep in a workflow the labs won't enter (compliance, physical ops, regulated data), own proprietary data/distribution, or sit *around* the labs' products (safety, cost, verification).

## B. Top 10 anger/pain themes → counter-product

1. **AI slop everywhere.** Reddit mocked Microsoft's CEO for objecting to the word "slop" — "thousands of users… doubling down" ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Microsoft-CEO-doesn-t-want-people-talking-about-AI-slop-Reddit-pushes-back.1196576.0.html)); a 2026 consumer backlash write-up frames it as a "widespread movement" ([TeigaAI](https://www.teigatech.com/post/why-everyone-hates-ai-slop-the-2026-consumer-backlash-explained)). Builder-side proof: GitHub anti-slop skills are among the fastest-growing repos of 2026 — [taste-skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill) 83k stars, [hallmark](https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark) 27.8k, [stop-slop](https://github.com/hardikpandya/stop-slop) 16.7k, [no-ai-slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop) 6.6k, [anti-slop](https://github.com/dmmulroy/anti-slop) lint rules 4k. → *Counter-product: taste/quality gating for AI output; feed filters (small [SlopMuter](https://github.com/diragb/slopmuter-auth-ms), [LinkedIn slop filter](https://github.com/akaukora/linkedin-slop-extension) projects exist, none has won).*
2. **"Guaranteed human" demand.** iHeartMedia found 90% of listeners want human-made media and launched a "guaranteed human" tag; 2026 tipped as "the year of anti-AI marketing" ([AOL/Fortune](https://www.aol.com/finance/why-2026-could-anti-ai-113058179.html)); Silicon Valley's "tone-deaf" response is itself a story ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2025/12/23/silicon-valleys-tone-deaf-take-on-the-ai-backlash-will-matter-in-2026/)). → *Human-provenance certification / labeling infrastructure.*
3. **Agents doing damage.** "An AI agent deleted our production database" hit 638 points / 794 comments on HN in April 2026 ([Happycapy](https://happycapy.ai/blog/ai-agent-deleted-db)); a Meta AI researcher's OpenClaw agent deleted her inbox despite stop commands; Moltbook's exposed DB let agents be hijacked ([MindStudio](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/ai-agent-database-wipe-disaster-lessons/)). Claude Code's tracker has 168 issues matching destructive actions, e.g. "[Agent deleted 2,229 untracked source files](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/23913)", "[ran git clean -fd without permission](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/45974)", "[sent personal file to a 3rd-party API without confirmation](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/56739)". → *Sandboxes/guardrails: [clawk](https://github.com/clawkwork/clawk) ("give coding agents a disposable Linux VM, not your laptop", 1k stars in 2 months), [NVIDIA SkillSpector](https://github.com/NVIDIA/SkillSpector) skill-security scanner 15.5k stars, [omnigent](https://github.com/omnigent-ai/omnigent) policy/sandbox 9.6k.*
4. **Voice-clone scams.** 1 in 4 Americans got a deepfake voice call in 12 months; FTC logged 250k+ AI voice-scam complaints in Q1 2026; elderly losses $2.3B ([cybrvault](https://cybrvault.com/blog/ai-voice-scams-2026-deepfake-phone-calls), [unboxfuture](https://www.unboxfuture.com/2026/05/the-rise-of-ai-voice-cloning-scams-in.html?m=1)). Advice is still "agree a family safe phrase" ([theslowai](https://theslowai.substack.com/p/protect-family-deepfake-voice-scams)) — i.e. no product. GitHub shows only academic detectors ([Veritas](https://github.com/EricTan7/Veritas), [GenD](https://github.com/yermandy/GenD)) and a Resemble [agent skill](https://github.com/resemble-ai/detect-skill). → *Consumer/family call-screening and verification.*
5. **AI companions/therapy considered unsafe.** Yara AI's founder shut it down deciding chatbots "aren't safe enough for people in real mental health crises"; China ordered Doubao/Qwen to kill anthropomorphic agent features July 2026 ([graveyard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mixtpatrik/killedbyai/main/graveyard.json)). → *Human-in-loop or clinician-supervised tools, not autonomous companions.*
6. **Job loss / replaced-by-AI.** Adobe tried to kill Animate to "refocus on AI" and was forced to reverse by backlash; TV Time was shut to chase AI ([graveyard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mixtpatrik/killedbyai/main/graveyard.json)). → *Tools that visibly keep humans in the loop and prove it.*
7. **Privacy / cloud dependence.** r/LocalLLaMA (266k members) cites privacy as the #1 motive — contracts, medical records, proprietary code ([aitooldiscovery](https://www.aitooldiscovery.com/guides/local-llm-reddit)); a "credible private alternative to ChatGPT" is a 2026 prediction ([ashrust](https://ashrust.substack.com/p/ai-trends-for-2026)). OpenClaw ("your assistant, on your devices… own-your-data") reached 388k stars in 9 months ([repo](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw)); Osaurus ("agents that run 100% locally on your Mac") charted on Product Hunt ([PH](https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard/monthly/2026/7)). → *Local-first vertical apps.*
8. **Model churn / rug-pulls.** Free tiers killed within 48 hours (MiniMax, Qwen Code), GitHub Models retired, forced auto-upgrades, DeepSeek renaming API endpoints "to death" ([graveyard](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mixtpatrik/killedbyai/main/graveyard.json)). → *Model-abstraction, regression testing, and cost/usage visibility (Claude Code users upvoted a "[5-hour window usage visibility](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-code/issues/9617)" request).*
9. **AI-written code quality.** HN June/July 2026 trend reports: "visible fatigue around tools that create messy output… whether all this code generation is actually worth the mess" ([mean.ceo July](https://blog.mean.ceo/hacker-news-trends-july-2026/), [June](https://blog.mean.ceo/hacker-news-trends-june-2026/)). Prelint ("prevent product drift in AI-written code") launched on PH ([PH](https://www.producthunt.com/leaderboard/monthly/2026/7)); [desloppify](https://github.com/peteromallet/desloppify) 3k stars. → *Verification layers for agent output.*
10. **Bubble skepticism.** HN threads "[The AI bubble is popping; we just don't know it yet](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49154601)", "[2026, the Last Year of the AI Bubble](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47030183)", "[What happens after the AI bubble bursts?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=47034849)"; even Sequoia notes AI has "two killer apps in coding and ChatGPT" relative to capex ([Sequoia](https://sequoiacap.com/article/ai-in-2026-the-tale-of-two-ais/)). → *Businesses that are profitable on day one, not capex-dependent.*

## E. Five strongest idea signals

1. **Blast-radius control for agents ("seatbelt for OpenClaw/Claude Code").** Pitch: a drop-in policy + snapshot/rollback layer that makes any agent's destructive action reversible and auditable. Why: the loudest HN thread of 2026 was an agent deleting prod; Claude Code has 168 destructive-action issues; clawk/SkillSpector/omnigent growth shows builders pay attention but nobody owns *recovery*. Labs ship permissions, not undo.
2. **Family voice-scam shield.** Pitch: on-device call screening that verifies a caller against enrolled family voices and challenges with a safe-phrase protocol, sold to adult children of elderly parents. Why: 250k FTC complaints in one quarter, $2.3B losses, and the state of the art is "pick a password"; detectors exist only as papers.
3. **"Guaranteed human" provenance for creators and brands.** Pitch: verifiable human-authorship attestations (capture-time signing, process logs) with a badge platforms and buyers trust. Why: 90% of listeners want human-made media, brands are marketing anti-AI, and platforms are cracking down on slop with no supply of positive proof.
4. **Local-first vertical AI for sensitive-document professions.** Pitch: an offline, on-device assistant for one profession that handles regulated files (e.g., immigration lawyers, therapists' notes, accountants) — no cloud, no model churn. Why: privacy is r/LocalLLaMA's #1 motive, OpenClaw's 388k stars prove appetite for "own your data" agents, and labs' cloud products can't credibly promise it. Avoid FDA-gated health.
5. **Model-churn insurance for teams shipping on LLM APIs.** Pitch: regression suite + behavior snapshot + auto-migration that detects when a forced upgrade or deprecation changes your outputs, with spend visibility. Why: 31 model deprecations in 20 months, 48-hour free-tier kills, and top-upvoted Claude Code asks are about visibility — the pain is recurring, B2B, and orthogonal to what the labs will ever build.
