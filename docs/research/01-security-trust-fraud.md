## 3. Startup ideas

### Idea 1 — Evidence-grade capture + AI-fraud screening for P&C claims
**Pitch:** "Notarized reality" for insurance: a capture SDK (signed at sensor, C2PA + device attestation + timing/geo) plus a forensic screening pipeline that scores every incoming claim photo/document for synthetic or manipulated content.
**Customer:** P&C carriers, TPAs, claims-adjustment apps. **Why now:** Verisk 98/99% stats; IFB 2026 priority; Rule 707/901(c) still unsettled, so courts and insurers need private provenance ([Enjuris](https://www.enjuris.com/deepfakes-legal-evidence-rule-707/)).
**Competitors:** Truepic (capture), SAS (in-house pipeline), CheckFile, Verisk. Moderately crowded on detection; whitespace on end-to-end capture→adjudication→litigation-ready audit trail.
**Lab immunity:** Labs generate; they don't attest sensors or integrate claims systems. **Moat:** carrier integrations, fraud-outcome data, chain-of-custody credibility.
**Scores:** Market 8 · Timing 8 · Lab-immunity 9 · Whitespace 6 · Moat 7 · Capital efficiency 6 · Demand 8.

### Idea 2 — State-law compliance layer for companion/therapy/edtech chatbots
**Pitch:** Drop-in SDK + reporting service: AI-disclosure cadence (3-hour minor reminders), evidence-based suicidal-ideation detection, crisis referral, age-assurance orchestration, and the annual CA Office of Suicide Prevention report (due July 2027), mapped across CA/OR/WA/NE and the GUARD Act.
**Customer:** thousands of long-tail companion/character/mental-health apps and any consumer chatbot. **Why now:** SB 243 has a private right of action; 98 bills in 2026 ([Skadden](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2025/10/new-california-companion-chatbot-law)).
**Competitors:** Persona (age assurance), Limina (content), GRC generalists. No dominant vertical player found.
**Lab immunity:** Obligations sit on the *operator*, not the model; labs won't file state reports for third-party apps. **Moat:** regulatory mapping, clinical validation of detection protocols, reporting history.
**Scores:** Market 6 · Timing 9 · Lab-immunity 8 · Whitespace 8 · Moat 6 · Capital efficiency 8 · Demand 7.

### Idea 3 — Scam-victim intervention platform for banks, exchanges, and wallets ("Operation Level Up as a service")
**Pitch:** Detect customers *being* scammed (romance/investment/pig-butchering) from behavioral + on-chain + contact-center signals, then run structured "break-the-spell" interventions, family alerts, and evidence packs for SARs and reimbursement decisions.
**Customer:** banks/credit unions, crypto exchanges, neobanks. **Why now:** FBI's Level Up notified 8,935 victims, 77% unaware, saving ~$562M — proof intervention works; $7.2B crypto-investment losses; reimbursement regimes shifting liability to FIs ([FactFind](https://www.factfind.com/factfind-brief/fraud-trends-scams/pig-butchering-crypto-scams-what-investigators-need-to-know-in-2026)).
**Competitors:** Elliptic/Chainalysis (wallet flags), Sardine/Feedzai (transaction risk), Carefull/EverSafe (elder-only). The victim-side intervention workflow is largely whitespace.
**Lab immunity:** Requires FI integrations, regulated data, outreach ops. **Moat:** cross-institution scam-pattern network, outcome data.
**Scores:** Market 8 · Timing 8 · Lab-immunity 9 · Whitespace 7 · Moat 7 · Capital efficiency 5 · Demand 8.

### Idea 4 — Deepfake-proof out-of-band verification network for SMB finance, sold via MSPs
**Pitch:** A shared "verified callback" rail: any payment change, wire, or credential reset triggers cryptographic verification to a pre-enrolled device of the real executive/vendor — replacing the "family safe word" with infrastructure, packaged for MSPs.
**Competitors:** Trustmi (enterprise AP), Nametag (helpdesk), CertifID (real estate). Crowded at enterprise; SMB/MSP channel underserved ([Guardz](https://guardz.com/blog/msp-cybersecurity-statistics-2026/)).
**Scores:** Market 7 · Timing 8 · Lab-immunity 9 · Whitespace 5 · Moat 7 · Capital efficiency 7 · Demand 8.

### Idea 5 — Neutral model-theft & distillation forensics
**Pitch:** Third-party fingerprinting/attestation service that proves whether a deployed model was derived (fine-tuned, merged, distilled) from a licensed weight set — for licensors, enterprises, and litigation.
**Why now:** ICLR/AAAI 2026 methods and LeafBench (696 model pairs) show fingerprints still fail under real transformations — a research-to-product gap with no startups ([OpenReview](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=Kan6Z0zzZi), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.10794)).
**Scores:** Market 5 · Timing 6 · Lab-immunity 6 · Whitespace 9 · Moat 6 · Capital efficiency 7 · Demand 5.

### Idea 6 — Article 50 deployer-side disclosure & mark-survivability compliance
**Pitch:** Inventory every AI touchpoint facing EU users, inject/verify disclosures, and continuously test whether machine-readable marks (C2PA/SynthID) survive the enterprise's own CMS/CDN/social pipelines; produce audit evidence.
**Why now:** live since 2 Aug 2026, marking grace ends 2 Dec 2026; "the screenshot problem remains unsolved" ([Eyesift](https://www.eyesift.com/faq/c2pa-content-credentials-2026-cryptographic-provenance-adoption/)).
**Scores:** Market 6 · Timing 9 · Lab-immunity 7 · Whitespace 5 · Moat 4 · Capital efficiency 8 · Demand 6.

## 4. Lanes to avoid (lab- or incumbent-exposed)
Generic agent runtime security/NHI (over $3.6B funded, Palo Alto/CrowdStrike/Google-Wiz consolidating); AI pentesting (labs' own models are the frontier — Astra); pure deepfake detection classifiers (arms race labs win); consumer elder-scam call apps (Scammer Guardian, SeniorShield, ZoraSafe, Jortty, state programs; and on-device platform scam detection by Google/Apple); proof-of-personhood (World/OpenAI-adjacent); KYA protocols (Visa/Mastercard set them).

## 5. Ranked shortlist
1. **Scam-victim intervention for FIs** (Idea 3) — biggest pool, proven intervention efficacy, lab-immune.
2. **Evidence-grade claims capture/screening** (Idea 1) — clearest budget owner, hard integration moat.
3. **Chatbot state-law compliance SDK** (Idea 2) — cheapest to start, strongest timing, real whitespace.
4. **SMB verified-callback network via MSPs** (Idea 4).
5. **Model-theft forensics** (Idea 5) — highest whitespace, smallest market today.
6. **Art. 50 deployer compliance** (Idea 6) — good wedge, weak moat.

**Verdict:** The sector's capital is piling into detection and agent-runtime security, exactly where model updates and platform consolidation erode startups fastest. The durable whitespace is in *process, provenance, and obligation* — products that sit between regulated institutions, victims, and legal deadlines, where a better model changes nothing. Pick a regulated buyer with a fresh statutory or liability trigger (FI reimbursement, SB 243, Art. 50, insurance fraud), and build the workflow plus data network, not the classifier.
