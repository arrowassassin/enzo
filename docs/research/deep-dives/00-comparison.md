## Verdict

**Pick by what you want to build.**

- **For a venture-scale company: the eldercare authority agent.** It is the only one of the three with a $1B comparable doing the same job (Solace, billing Medicare for navigation), a priced willingness-to-pay ladder from $8 to $400 a month, a tailwind that cannot slip, and a moat the labs just validated by absorbing everything around it. Its cheapest path to $1M ARR of the three. The wedge is narrow and specific: "call Medicare, insurers and pharmacies for my parent" at $79–99 a month with a human desk, framed as the instrument of the human POA agent, instrumented for Medicare navigation billing from day one. Do not start with scam screening or fraud monitoring; those are free now.
- **For fastest revenue with least capital: physical-AI compliance.** CRA incident reporting goes live in nine days and no GRC tool covers connected machinery. A €2–3M seed reaches $1M ARR on ~60 OEM customers. The lab is a real venture option, but treat it as a paid evidence-pack service through a TÜV or Intertek partnership before pursuing accreditation. Kill signal: a Machinery Regulation postponement in 2027 or a Vanta CRA module before December 2027.
- **Provenance only as a narrow B2B compliance product.** Ship an EU deployer "disclosure survivability audit" before 2 December 2026 and a Steam/Unreal per-asset ledger in Q1 2027. Do not build the registry and do not sell to creators or students.

**If forced to a single answer: the eldercare authority agent.** It has the largest market, the strongest priced evidence, the lowest technical risk, the lowest capital to first revenue, and the only path to a $1B outcome. Its risks are execution and trust, not absorption, which is the risk profile you want when the brief is "something a lab cannot erase."
