## 2. Startup ideas

Scores: Market / Timing / Lab-immunity / Whitespace / Moat / Capital-eff / Demand.

### Idea A — "Verified Human" provenance rail for independent creators
**Pitch:** One identity-bound certification + C2PA-style signing layer that lets indie musicians, podcasters, webtoon artists and writers prove human authorship once and carry it across Spotify, Deezer, YouTube, Webtoon, AO3, radio. Sell to platforms as fraud/slop filtering, to creators as a badge, to brands/radio (iHeart-style) as sourcing.
**Customer:** Indie creators (badge, $5–15/yr), DSPs/radio/publishers (API).
**Why now:** Deezer >50% AI uploads/85% fraud; Spotify badges; Authors Guild seal already selling at $10; EU Art. 50 live since Aug 2026.
**Competitors:** Authors Guild (books only), Human Made Mark (film), Not By AI badge ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No-AI_label)), per-platform badges — all siloed, none cross-platform.
**Lab-proof:** Labs are the thing being certified against; they cannot credibly run the registry. Platform risk (Spotify builds its own) is real but every platform doing it separately is the wedge.
**Moat:** Registry network effects, trust brand, platform integrations.
**Scores:** 6 / 9 / 9 / 6 / 6 / 8 / 7.

### Idea B — Licensed-IP fan-creation "walled gardens" for mid-tier rights holders
**Pitch:** White-label, licensed roleplay/story/short-video platform for anime publishers, webtoon studios, K-pop agencies, game IP and book series — the Udio/UMG walled-garden model applied to characters, with revenue share and non-exportable outputs.
**Customer:** IP owners (B2B2C), superfans (subscriptions).
**Why now:** Disney–OpenAI collapsed with Sora, leaving the "official fan AI" slot empty ([Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/business/2026/03/25/tech/openai-sora-disney-deal/)); Character.AI (20M MAU) is bleeding users over ads/moderation; Midjourney litigation forces rights holders to want a sanctioned alternative.
**Competitors:** Character.AI, Janitor, Showrunner/Fable (Amazon-backed, original worlds) ([Variety](https://variety.com/2025/digital/news/netflix-of-ai-amazon-invests-fable-showrunner-launch-1236471989/)); nobody aggregates licensed mid-tier IP.
**Lab-proof:** Labs won't sign hundreds of small IP deals and just proved they abandon consumer media products; the moat is the contract book, not the model.
**Moat:** Exclusive licences, fan community, rights-holder trust. Risk: backlash means it must be opt-in and clearly labelled.
**Scores:** 7 / 8 / 8 / 7 / 8 / 5 / 7.

### Idea C — AI-persona talent agency + live-ops studio (VTuber/streamer)
**Pitch:** Build, run and monetise a roster of AI-driven live personalities (Twitch/YouTube/TikTok Live) with human showrunners, community managers and merch/sponsorship — the Hololive of AI streamers.
**Why now:** Neuro-sama tops Twitch subs; AI VTubers "earning millions" ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/07/02/ai-virtual-personality-youtubers-or-vtubers-are-earning-millions.html)); VTuber market $3–7B ([abovea](https://abovea.tech/vtuber-statistics-trends/)); Brave group raised ¥8B+ for expansion.
**Competitors:** Channel (a16z), Hedra ($32M) for tooling, Brave group/Hololive (human talent). Few run AI-native rosters.
**Lab-proof:** Live 24/7 ops, parasocial community, character IP and show formats are outside lab scope; models are commodity inputs.
**Scores:** 6 / 8 / 9 / 6 / 7 / 7 / 8.

### Idea D — Independent-artist opt-in AI licensing collective (a "PRO for AI")
**Pitch:** Aggregate stems, voices and catalogs from indies/small labels into one rights-cleared, metadata-rich pool; license to Suno/Udio/Stability/game and ad-music buyers; pay per-use royalties and offer voice-model opt-in.
**Why now:** Majors have deals; indies are excluded and the MAC is demanding terms; Suno needs licensed data for 2026 models; LANDR's "Fair Trade AI" shows the appetite ([LANDR](https://www.landr.com/ai-music-tools)).
**Competitors:** LANDR Fair Trade, label-run deals, Vermillio/Loti (likeness, not catalog). Whitespace on the indie catalog side.
**Lab-proof:** Labs/AI firms are customers; they cannot manufacture consent.
**Scores:** 7 / 8 / 8 / 6 / 7 / 6 / 6.

### Idea E — Long-tail sports auto-broadcast with AI commentary and fan-personalised highlights
**Why now:** Sportway raised €20M for 250k matches/yr ([Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2026/06/23/from-the-sidelines-to-250000-matches-a-year-sportway-raises-eur20m-to-scale-its-ai-sports-media-platform/)); H1 2025 sports-tech deals ~$52B ([Growth List](https://growthlist.co/list-of-funded-sports-startups/)).
**Competitors:** Pixellot ($218M), Hudl ($229M), Veo, Sportway — crowded.
**Scores:** 8 / 6 / 10 / 3 / 6 / 4 / 8. Big and defensible, but late and capital-heavy.

### Idea F — Lower-capex avatar residencies for legacy acts and estates
**Why now:** ABBA Voyage: 3.5M tickets, ~$2M/week; $500M NYC venue approved ([Propmodo](https://propmodo.com/abbas-hologram-concert-heads-to-midtown-west/)); Loti/Vermillio make estate consent contractable.
**Scores:** 6 / 6 / 10 / 8 / 8 / 2 / 6. Capex is the killer; needs a venue partner.

### Anti-patterns (avoid)
Personalised kids' stories (Meta StoryKit), AI podcast generators (NotebookLM has four formats), AI-native social feeds (Sora dead, Moltbook absorbed by Meta), generic dubbing (ElevenLabs $11B, YouTube native), streaming discovery (Netflix + OpenAI search) — all lab- or platform-owned.

## 3. Ranked shortlist
1. **A — Verified Human provenance rail** (timing 9, lab-immunity 9, cheap).
2. **B — Licensed mid-tier IP fan gardens** (largest upside; contract moat).
3. **D — Indie AI licensing collective** (structural gap majors created).
4. **C — AI-persona talent agency** (proven demand, execution-heavy).
5. **E — Long-tail sports** (big, defensible, crowded).
6. **F — Avatar residencies** (great moat, brutal capex).

## 4. Verdict
The labs have effectively conceded consumer entertainment products (Sora shut, Disney deal dead) while their models commoditise generation, so the value has shifted to the three things a model update cannot supply: consent/licences, verified humanness, and live community. The strongest whitespace sits where 2025–26 label and platform deals left gaps — independent artists, mid-tier IP holders, and cross-platform human verification — each with a visible catalyst in the last 90 days. Sector risk is backlash volatility: any product must be opt-in and loudly labelled, or fandoms will boycott it as they did WIT Studio and the webtoon contest.
