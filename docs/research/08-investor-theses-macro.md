## D. Explicit "don't build" warnings

- **Cost-reduction wrappers**: "The strongest AI companies aren't cost-reduction stories — they're revenue-reinforcement stories" (Haber, a16z).
- **Horizontal/no-code apps**: "Our agents never even suggest these products, that's the danger" (20VC, Aug 2026); unprofitable infra "faces elimination during corrections"; 95% projected failure rate ([20VC](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/javier-jaime/Second-Brain/main/podcasts_briefings/LHR_2026-03_20VC.md)).
- **Seat-priced SaaS**: Bain — one martech client's AI costs +349% vs revenue +38%; outcome pricing displacing seats ([Bain](https://www.bain.com/insights/ai-brings-headwinds-and-tailwinds-to-the-rule-of-40/)). YC's Friedman: legacy SaaS moats are gone (RFS S26).
- **Agent-bait content**: Zhang (a16z) warns of keyword-stuffing-style spam aimed at agents — a trust problem, not a product.
- **Thin GPT/Claude wrappers, consumer AI without monetization, "AI for everything" platforms** (synthesis of YC/a16z/Bessemer/Foundation, [mirror](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dkittelson/sentinel/main/research/2026_startup_opportunities.md)).
- **Lab absorption signals (inference from primary events)**: MCP 2026-07-28 deleted session/sampling plumbing; Apple ships free on-device 7B + tool calling; OpenAI cut prices 80% and acquired Promptfoo (evals). Anything that is "a model, a session layer, or a price arbitrage" gets absorbed.
- **Jobs backlash**: Amodei's 50%/10–20% warning, Suleyman's "will be automated within two years" ([BI](https://www.businessinsider.com/microsoft-ai-ceo-mustafa-suleyman-white-collar-tasks-automation-prediction-2026-2)) show political risk for overt labor-replacement positioning; Altman's counter: "people really care about other people" ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/columns/openai-ceo-sam-altman-ai-in-hollywood-will-get-people-to-care-more-about-human-creators-not-less-1236726413/)).

## E. Top 6 for a solo Rust/systems founder with little capital

1. **Stateless MCP + x402/TAP server toolkit in Rust** — "Make any API agent-callable, agent-payable, and self-updating in one binary." Why: YC "Software for Agents" and "Self-Maintaining APIs"; MCP 2026-07-28 forces a rewrite; x402 has 165M txns but the Rust impl has ~288 stars ([x402-rs](https://github.com/x402-rs/x402-rs)).
2. **Provenance/labeling compliance edge** — "C2PA signing + AI-content detection as a Cloudflare-style sidecar." Why: EU Art. 50 live Aug 2 2026, CA SB 942; YC "Proving You're Human"; Pangram's $9M shows demand.
3. **Local-first agent runtime** — "goose-class agent OS that runs Llama 5/DeepSeek V4/Qwen3.8 on one workstation, no cloud." Why: open-weight parity, gpt-oss-20b in 16GB, Apple Foundation 2 free, EU data-sovereignty moat; YC "Cloud for Small Software"; block's goose is already local-first under AAIF.
4. **Deterministic verification layer for agent actions** — "Sandboxed replay + property checks before an agent's write hits production." Why: Probably ($9M, a16z), Amazon's senior sign-off rule after outages, 20VC's "verification as a service" framing; picks-and-shovels sold to labs and enterprises.
5. **Robot data/teleop pipeline** — "Low-latency capture, sync and annotation pipe for embodied-AI labs." Why: XDOF $70M, Avea Robotics (YC S26 teleop), YC "Data for the Real World", 32 robotics cos in S26; a Rust engineer can own the pipe without owning robots.
6. **Tamper-evident ADMT audit log** — "Attested decision ledger for Colorado SB 189 (Jan 2027) and EU Annex III (Dec 2027)." Why: YC "AI-Native Compliance"; Onyx $113M/Actualyze $7M show governance spend; regulation narrowed to *disclosure*, which is exactly a logging product.

Crowded — avoid as a solo: agent identity (NewCore $66M, Oak $60M, Hush $30M), voice AI, coding agents.
