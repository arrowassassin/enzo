## 3. Ideas

### A. Compliance back office for specialty subcontractors
**Pitch:** Certified payroll, prevailing-wage, lien waivers, COI tracking and pay-app packaging for electrical/mechanical subs, ingesting from Procore/Sage/Foundation. **Customer:** 20–300-employee subs on public/infra work. **Why now:** infra spend and Dili's raise show compliance is a budget line; Trayd validates the wedge but is payroll-first. **Competitors:** Trayd, Karmen, ContextFort, Procore add-ons — moderate. **Lab-proof:** state-by-state wage rules, form versions, GC portal integrations; labs won't build these. **Moat:** becomes the system of record for compliance evidence. **Fit:** document pipelines suit the founder; needs a construction-ops co-founder. **Scores:** market 6, timing 7, defensibility 8, fit 6, capital 7.

### B. Legacy-interop engine for post-acute care (home health, hospice, DME)
**Pitch:** "Tennr + HeyDonto Conduit for post-acute": fax/HL7/X12 837/835/270/271 and EVV ingestion into a workflow system of record. **Customer:** regional agencies and DME suppliers. **Why now:** Tennr ($605M) proved fax-intake; HeyDonto got $200M seed valuation on EDI bridging; MochaCare (YC W26) is only doing scheduling. **Competitors:** Tennr (may expand), Alora/WellSky incumbents — low-moderate. **Lab-proof:** protocol cruft and payer-specific edits are unglamorous; HIPAA BAAs and Medicaid EVV mandates. **Moat:** integrations + claims-edit data. **Fit:** highest — parsers, state machines, reliability; Rust is an asset. Clinical/billing co-founder essential. **Scores:** market 7, timing 8, defensibility 8, fit 8, capital 6.

### C. Shop-side collision estimate and supplement intelligence
**Pitch:** Reads CCC/Mitchell/Audatex estimates plus photos, flags missed OEM procedures, drafts supplements and insurer-DRP arguments. **Customer:** independent body shops. **Why now:** VaraFix launched Aug 25 2026 into an empty shop-side niche ([CollisionWeek](https://collisionweek.com/2026/08/25/varafix-launches-ai-estimate-review-platform/)); Tractable ($200M+) sits insurer-side ([Autobody News](https://www.autobodynews.com/news/shop-owners-have-options-in-adding-collision-repair-ai)). **Competitors:** VaraFix, AutoTechIQ — low. **Lab-proof:** OEM repair-procedure licensing, estimating-platform integrations, insurer rule data. **Moat:** supplement-outcome dataset. **Fit:** computer vision + document parsing suit GPU skills; needs an estimator co-founder. **Scores:** market 5, timing 7, defensibility 7, fit 7, capital 8.

### D. Sovereign on-prem document AI for EU mid-market law/tax
**Pitch:** GPU-efficient, on-prem inference appliance plus workflow layer for German/Nordic firms that can't use US clouds. **Why now:** Noxtua €80.7M on exactly this thesis; Doctrine buying Maite.ai for Spain. **Competitors:** Noxtua, Legora, Doctrine — rising. **Lab-proof:** labs don't ship weights on-prem; regulation is the moat. **Risk:** infra without workflow is not a system of record. **Fit:** best pure-technical match (Rust/GPU), weakest distribution. **Scores:** market 6, timing 6, defensibility 6, fit 8, capital 5.

### E. CA-firm practice OS for India (WhatsApp → Tally/GST → filings)
**Pitch:** Sell to chartered-accountant firms, not SMBs; they are the trusted distribution channel. **Why now:** Qosh already turns WhatsApp docs into Tally vouchers; Tally AI cuts GST time 8–12h to 2–3h/month ([Refrens](https://www.refrens.com/en-in/top-ai-accounting-tools)); SMB tools price at ₹500–5K/month ([AI Tech News](https://aitechnews.in/ai-tools-indian-small-business-2026-whatsapp-gst-automation/)). **Competitors:** Zoho, Vyapar, NextBill, Qosh — crowded at SMB tier, thinner at firm tier. **Lab-proof:** GSTN portal integration, Tally lock-in, local regulation cadence. **Fit:** only works if the founder is India-based with CA relationships. **Scores:** market 7, timing 7, defensibility 5, fit 5, capital 9.

**Verdict.** Vertical AI is now the best-funded application category, but the obvious wedges (scribes, prior auth, dental, freight voice, big-law) are already at $600M–$5B valuations and the labs are shipping direct healthcare connectors, so a new entrant must anchor on legacy protocols, regulation, and becoming the system of record rather than on model quality. For this founder, the strongest fit is the boring integration layer (fax/EDI/EVV/estimating platforms) in a second-tier vertical where incumbents are 20 years old and no YC company has planted a flag. None of these should be started without a domain co-founder; the technical moat is real but distribution and trust are what close the first ten customers.
